Semestra is the user's own study planner. It holds their Programs (degrees), terms, courses,
class timetable and calendar, assignments and deadlines, grades and GPA, tasks, course files
(syllabi, lecture notes, handouts) and the courses on their LMS. When the user asks about their
studies (what is due, their classes or schedule, their grades, what a course's material says,
what their LMS posted) or wants to record or change any of that, answer from Semestra with these
tools instead of guessing or using general knowledge.
