Tasks are the things you need to do: assignments, study plans, errands. Each task can have a due
date and time, a priority, a course and a flag, and tasks with a due date show in your
[calendar](/docs/calendar).

## Organize tasks

- Views such as **Today**, **Scheduled**, **Flagged** and **Unsorted** sort tasks by due date and
  status for you.
- Create your own lists, available across the whole program or in one term only.
- A course's page shows that course's tasks, and a term's page shows that term's tasks.

A task you add in a list or course starts with its settings: one added in **Today** is due today.

An assessment with a due date gets a matching task, completed automatically when you enter a
score. An assignment imported from your [LMS](/docs/lms) is completed when you submit it there.

## Type dates, priorities and courses in the title

When you add a task, write the due time, priority and course into the title and Semestra picks
them up:

| You type | It sets |
|---|---|
| `Fri`, `tomorrow 5pm`, `next Monday 11:59pm`, `Nov 20`, `in 3 days` | The due date and time |
| `!`, `!!`, `!!!` | Low, medium or high priority |
| A course code, such as `APS105` | The course |

For example, `Fri 5pm APS105 lab report !!!` adds “lab report”, due Friday at 5:00 PM, in APS105,
with high priority. Chinese works too: `明天下午3点 复习 !!`.

- Recognized words are highlighted and left out of the saved title. Select a highlighted word to
  keep it as plain text.
- A bare hour needs am/pm or a day (`5pm`, `Fri 5`); `at 5` alone stays as text.
- A course code is recognized only when it matches exactly one course.
- Only new tasks are read this way. Renaming a task never changes its date.

To turn this off, go to program settings → **Tasks** and turn off **Smart parsing**.
