A sticky note is a card you can put on the overview of a Program, term or course, or on the Today
page. Add one from the page's card editing; you can have as many as you like.

## Writing

A note reads like a note: click a link to open it, and tick a checklist item to mark it done.
Long notes scroll inside the card.

To write, click the pencil button in the note's top right corner, or double-click the text (an
empty note starts as soon as you click it). There is nothing to save. While you write, a small
toolbar appears at the bottom of the note for bold, italic, lists, checklists, links and tables.
Typing `- ` starts a list and `[ ] ` a checklist item. Click outside the note, press Esc or click
the check button to finish.

While writing, clicking a link lets you edit it; ⌘-click (Ctrl-click on Windows) opens it, and so
does the button next to the address in the link box.

## Tables

Choose **Insert table** in the toolbar to add a small table. With the cursor in a table, the same
button opens a menu to add or delete rows and columns, or to delete the table. A wide table
scrolls sideways.

You can also type a table in Markdown: write the header row, such as `| Chapter | Status |`, then
`| --- | --- |` on the next line and press Enter. Pasted Markdown, tables included, is formatted
too.

## Color and size

Pick the note's color from the card's settings. A small note has no toolbar; make the card larger
to format text.

## With Sem

Ask [Sem](/docs/sem) to write something down ("note the midterm scope in this course's sticky
note"). It adds to the end of the note; you can also ask it to tidy up the whole note.
