Sem is the assistant in Semestra. It needs an [AI provider](/docs/ai) with a model for the **Sem**
purpose.

## Conversations

Sem's page lists your conversations in a column beside the open one, pinned first and then by
day, with a search box and **New conversation** at the top. Rename, pin or delete a conversation
from its **⋯** menu. On a narrow window the list moves under the **History** button.

## Suggested questions

Above the input of a new conversation, Sem lists a few questions you can click, drawn from your
data: assessments and tasks that are due soon or overdue, classes still to come today, courses
with no assessments yet, and courses with some grades in. A course a question names is sent to
Sem as a reference.

With a [Light tasks](/docs/ai) model set up, Semestra has it reword these questions, also taking
your recent conversation titles into account, at most twice a day per program. Turn off
**Personalized suggestions** in global settings → **AI → Sem → Conversations** to keep the fixed
wording and make no model calls.

## Questions from Sem

When Sem needs your choice to go on, for example which of two courses you mean or which evenings
suit you for studying, it asks in place of the input box: a question with a few options, one of
them sometimes marked as recommended. Pick an option (or press its number), type your own answer,
or skip the question.

## Approving changes

Sem reads freely. Before it creates or changes something, it shows the change in place of the
input box, and nothing happens until you approve it. You can also edit the values first, or type
what Sem should do instead. When several changes wait, move between them or approve them all. Global settings → **AI → Sem → Approvals** sets
which changes Sem may make without asking: **Ask every time**, **By module**, or **Auto-approve
all**. Deleting always needs your approval.

## Memory

Sem remembers lasting facts about you, how you want it to work, your plans, and where to find
things. Review and edit them in global settings → **AI → Sem → Memory**. A **Temporary chat** neither
reads nor saves memories.

## Long conversations

When the conversation nearly fills the model's context, Sem compacts it before answering: it
summarizes earlier messages and keeps the latest ones word for word. To do it yourself, type
`/compact`, optionally followed by what the summary must keep. While it runs you can't send a new
message.
