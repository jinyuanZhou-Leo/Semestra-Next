Sem is the AI assistant in Semestra. It can look up your courses, grades, tasks and course files,
and add or change things for you, asking first before every change. To use Sem, add a
[provider](/docs/ai) first.

## Start a conversation

Ask questions or give instructions in plain language, such as “What's due next week?” or “Record
82 for the MAT135 midterm.” A new conversation suggests a few questions based on your data; pick
one to get started.

You can pin, rename, search and delete conversations. A message you haven't sent, including its
attachments, stays with its conversation when you go to another page.

### Refer to courses and files

Type `@` to refer to a course, a term or a course file, and Sem answers with it in mind. You can
also drag files into the conversation as attachments.

### Use skills

Type `/` to pick a skill, a set of steps Sem follows for one job, such as recording grades,
importing from documents or answering from course files. You can pick several in one message.

### When Sem asks you

When Sem needs your choice to go on, for example which course you mean, it offers a few options
and sometimes marks the one it recommends. Pick one, type your own answer, or skip the question.

## Approve changes

Sem can read your data at any time. Before it adds or changes anything, it shows you the change,
and nothing happens until you approve it. You can edit the change first, or tell Sem to do it
differently.

To have Sem ask less often, choose an option in global settings → **AI → Sem → Approvals**:

- **Ask every time**: every change needs your approval.
- **By feature**: changes in the features you choose are approved automatically.
- **Auto-approve all**: every change happens right away.

Deleting always needs your approval.

## Memory

Sem remembers lasting things about you: your background and preferences, how you want it to work,
your plans, and where to find what you use often. Review, edit or delete them in global settings →
**AI → Sem → Memory**.

With **Auto Dream** on, Sem tidies its memories in the background at most once a day: it adds what
it learned from recent conversations, merges duplicates and removes what's out of date.

When a conversation shouldn't be remembered, start a **Temporary chat**. It neither reads nor saves
memories, and other conversations can't refer to it.

## Refer to earlier conversations

When you bring up something you discussed before (“What did we decide about the lab report?”),
Sem looks through your other conversations in the same program and links the one it used. To turn
this off, go to global settings → **AI → Sem → Memory** and turn off **Reference other
conversations**.

## Long conversations

When a conversation gets long, Sem summarizes the earlier part and keeps the latest messages as
they are, so you can keep going. To do it yourself, type `/compact`, optionally followed by what
the summary must keep.
