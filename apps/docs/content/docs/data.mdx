All of this is in global settings → **Data**, which also shows how much storage you use. To free
some space, delete course files you no longer need, or Sem conversations with attachments or
files mentioned with `@`.

## Back up

**Export all data** creates a `.zip` backup of your programs, terms, courses, grades, schedules,
tasks, settings and plugin data. Turn on **Include files** to add your uploaded files too. You can
download a backup for 24 hours after it's created.

<Callout type="warn">
  For your security, backups never include provider API keys, LMS access tokens or API tokens.
  Enter them again after restoring.
</Callout>

## Restore

<Steps>
<Step>

### Upload the backup

Drop the `.zip` on **Restore from backup** to see what it holds.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose what to import

- Import all programs, or only some of them.
- **Merge** adds the backup next to what you have. Importing the same backup twice gives you two
  copies.
- **Replace** deletes all your current data first. Type `replace my data` to confirm.
- **Also import preferences and layouts**: theme, language, page order, layouts, installed plugins
  and providers.

</Step>
<Step>

### Import

The import runs in the background and then lists what it imported and skipped. If it fails or you
stop it, nothing changes.

</Step>
</Steps>

You can restore backups from this version or an earlier one; a backup from a newer version needs
Semestra to be updated first. If a backup holds data for a feature this version doesn't have, that
part is skipped and the rest is imported.

## Delete your account

**Delete account** permanently deletes your account and everything in it, and can't be undone. Type
`delete my account` to confirm; if you signed in a while ago, you'll also confirm it's you.

You're signed out on every device at once. Your data is then deleted in the background, and you get
an email when it's gone. To keep anything, export a backup first.

You can delete a program you no longer need in its settings, but your account always keeps at
least one program.

Developers can export and restore over the API; see the [API reference](/docs/api/reference/portability).
