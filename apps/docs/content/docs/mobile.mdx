Semestra is built for a computer. On a phone (a screen narrower than 768 px, or a phone turned sideways) it opens a smaller
app with a tab bar at the bottom, made for checking your day, keeping up with tasks and asking Sem.

## What is on the phone

- **Today**: today's classes and events, and the tasks that are overdue or due in the next 7 days.
- **Tasks**: your tasks by due date, with the same quick add as on a computer (dates and `!`
  priority marks are understood). Tap a task to edit or delete it.
- **Sem**: everything Sem does on a computer, including confirmation cards, `@` references, `/`
  skills and attachments.
- **More**: switch between programs, open settings, and sign out.

The phone app opens on Today, or on Tasks or Sem if you chose one of them under **Settings → Preferences →
Navigation → Default page**. Other start pages only apply on a computer.

Grades, the calendar, files and imports stay on a computer. Opening one of their links on a
phone shows a note and a button back to Today. Links work on both: the same address opens the
phone version on a phone and the full page on a computer.

## Add it to your home screen

Semestra can be added to the home screen and then opens full screen, without the browser bar.

- **iPhone (Safari)**: tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. The home screen app keeps its
  own sign-in, so sign in once more inside it.
- **Android (Chrome)**: open the **⋮** menu and tap **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

An iPad is wide enough for the full version, so there the home screen app opens the same pages
as on a computer. There are no notifications on the web version.
