Semestra is built for a computer. On a phone it opens a simpler version made for checking your day,
keeping up with tasks and asking Sem.

## What you can do on a phone

- **Today**: today's classes and events, and tasks that are overdue or due in the next 7 days.
- **Tasks**: your tasks grouped by due date. Add, edit and delete tasks, and type dates and
  priorities straight into the title (see [Tasks](/docs/tasks)).
- **Sem**: everything Sem can do on a computer.
- **More**: switch programs, open settings and sign out.

Grades, the calendar, files and imports are available on a computer only. Links work on both: the
same address opens the phone version on a phone and the full page on a computer.

## Add to Home Screen

Added to your Home Screen, Semestra opens full screen, like an app.

- **iPhone (Safari)**: choose **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. Sign in once more after opening
  it from the Home Screen.
- **Android (Chrome)**: open the browser menu and choose **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

An iPad is wide enough for the full version. On iPhone and iPad, only Semestra added to the Home
Screen can receive [notifications](/docs/notifications).
