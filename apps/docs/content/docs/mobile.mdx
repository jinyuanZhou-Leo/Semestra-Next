Semestra is built for a computer. On a phone it opens a simpler version made for checking your day,
keeping up with tasks, looking at your courses and asking Sem.

## What you can do on a phone

- **Today**: today's classes and events, and tasks that are overdue or due in the next 7 days.
- **Tasks**: your tasks grouped by due date. Add, edit and delete tasks, and type dates and
  priorities straight into the title (see [Tasks](/docs/tasks)). A task that is an assignment on
  your LMS opens the assignment's details.
- **Courses**: this term's courses with their grades so far, under your cumulative and term GPA.
  Switch terms at the top right. Open a course to see:
  - **Overview**: coming classes and events, the grade so far and the course's tasks.
  - **Grades**: each assessment and its score. An assignment from your LMS opens its details and
    your teacher's feedback.
  - **Files**: browse folders or search by name, and open a file with a tap.
  - **Announcements**: the course's announcements on your LMS, marked as read when you open them.
- **Sem**: everything Sem can do on a computer.
- **More**: switch programs, open settings and sign out.

Editing grades, organizing files, the calendar and imports are available on a computer only. A
notification opens the course page it is about on a phone too. Links work on both: the
same address opens the phone version on a phone and the full page on a computer.

## Add to Home Screen

Added to your Home Screen, Semestra opens full screen, like an app.

- **iPhone (Safari)**: choose **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. Sign in once more after opening
  it from the Home Screen.
- **Android (Chrome)**: open the browser menu and choose **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

An iPad is wide enough for the full version. On iPhone and iPad, only Semestra added to the Home
Screen can receive [notifications](/docs/notifications).
