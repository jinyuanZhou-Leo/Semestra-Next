---
title: Connect your LMS
description: Connect your school's Canvas so courses, assignments and grades stay in sync.
---

Once your school's LMS is connected, you can import courses and assignments directly, and when
grades or due dates change on Canvas, Semestra follows. Canvas is supported today.

Semestra only reads from Canvas and doesn't change anything there. Only if you turn on **Allow
changes on the platform** in global settings → **LMS → Platform access** does it mark announcements
you read in Semestra as read on Canvas.

## 1. Add a connection

<Steps>
<Step>

In Canvas, open **Account → Settings** and choose **New access token** under _Approved
integrations_. Copy the token; Canvas shows it only once.

</Step>
<Step>

In global settings → **LMS**, add a connection, choose **Canvas**, and enter your school's Canvas
address (for example `https://school.instructure.com`) and the access token. Semestra checks that it
works before saving.

</Step>
</Steps>

Access tokens are stored encrypted. If a token expires or is revoked, the sidebar asks you to
reconnect.

New users can also add a connection during onboarding, then import courses from Canvas when
creating their first term.

### Sign in with a school account

Some self-hosted instances let you sign in to Canvas with your school account instead of creating
a token. When adding a connection, choose **School account**, enter your Canvas address, sign in on
your school's page, and paste back the full address of the page you end up on. That last page
saying “Page Not Found” is expected.

<Callout type="warn">
  This sign-in isn't one Canvas offers to third-party apps. It may be against your school's rules,
  may stop working at any time, and may sign you out of the Canvas app on your phone. Using it is
  your decision; if you're unsure, use an access token.
</Callout>

For how an administrator turns it on, see
[Self-hosting → Optional features](/docs/self-hosting/features#canvas-school-account-sign-in).

## 2. Choose a connection for a program

In program settings → **LMS**, choose the connection this program's courses use.

## 3. Link courses

Courses you [import from Canvas](./imports.mdx) are linked automatically. For an existing course,
choose its Canvas course in course settings → **LMS**.

To stop syncing a course, turn off **Sync this course**. It then no longer updates and its Canvas
calendar events are hidden, but you can still read it in the Canvas tab and ask Sem about it.

## Automatic sync

Linked courses sync in the background, without you opening anything. Change how often in global
settings → **LMS → Background sync**, or choose **Sync now** at any time.

- **Imported assignments follow Canvas**: when a name, due date, weight or grade changes on Canvas,
  Semestra updates it. Values you changed yourself aren't overwritten; if both sides changed, you
  choose which to keep. To review a kind of change first, turn it off in global settings → **LMS →
  Update automatically**.
- **New assignments wait for you**: new assignments on Canvas aren't added on their own; import or
  ignore them.
- **Assignments deleted on Canvas** are kept until you decide whether to delete them.
- **Tasks complete when you submit**: when you submit an assignment on Canvas, its task is
  completed.

Sync status is shown at the bottom of the sidebar: when it last ran, or how many items are waiting
for you. Open it to see what needs you and the recent updates. Updates from the last 14 days can be
undone.

Values you changed that no longer match Canvas are listed separately, and each can go back to the
Canvas value in one step.

## What else you get

- **Canvas tab**: read a course's announcements, assignments and grades, modules, pages, files and
  syllabus without leaving Semestra. Content uses easy-to-read typography by default, or Canvas's own
  style if you prefer.
- **Calendar**: Canvas calendar events appear in your term calendar.
- **Announcements card**: keep the latest announcements on a course or term page.
- **Ask Sem**: Sem can search and read everything in a course on Canvas, including assignment
  instructions, rubrics and your instructor's comments, and tell you about new announcements, new
  grades and what's due. Anything Sem brings in goes through an import you review first.

## Not supported yet

New Quizzes and discussion checkpoints can't be read yet.
