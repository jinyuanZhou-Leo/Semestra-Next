---
title: Connect your LMS
description: Connect your school's Canvas so courses, assignments and grades stay in sync.
---

Once your school's LMS is connected, you can import courses and assignments directly, and when
grades or due dates change on Canvas, Semestra follows. Canvas is supported today.

Semestra only reads from Canvas and doesn't change anything there. Only if you turn on **Allow
changes on the platform** in global settings → **LMS → Platform access** does it mark announcements
you read in Semestra as read on Canvas.

## 1. Add a connection

<Steps>
<Step>

In Canvas, open **Account → Settings** and choose **New access token** under _Approved
integrations_. Copy the token; Canvas shows it only once.

</Step>
<Step>

In global settings → **LMS**, add a connection, choose **Canvas**, and enter your school's Canvas
address (for example `https://school.instructure.com`) and the access token. Semestra checks that it
works before saving.

</Step>
<Step>

Choose how course materials sync: whether Canvas files come into your course files, and whether
they're read from Canvas or saved in Semestra (see [Course materials](/docs/lms/imports#course-materials)).
The first sync after you link courses follows this; you can change it later in settings.

</Step>
</Steps>

Access tokens are stored encrypted. If a token expires or is revoked, the sidebar asks you to
reconnect.

New users can also add a connection and choose how course materials sync during onboarding, then
import courses from Canvas when creating their first term.

### Sign in with a school account

Some self-hosted instances let you sign in to Canvas with your school account instead of creating
a token. When adding a connection, choose **School account**, enter your Canvas address, sign in on
your school's page, and paste back the full address of the page you end up on. That last page
saying “Page Not Found” is expected.

<Callout type="warn">
  This sign-in isn't one Canvas offers to third-party apps. It may be against your school's rules,
  may stop working at any time, and may sign you out of the Canvas app on your phone. Using it is
  your decision; if you're unsure, use an access token.
</Callout>

For how an administrator turns it on, see
[Self-hosting → Optional features](/docs/self-hosting/features#canvas-school-account-sign-in).

## 2. Choose a connection for a program

In program settings → **LMS**, choose the connection this program's courses use.

## 3. Link courses

Courses you [import from Canvas](./imports.mdx) are linked automatically. For an existing course,
choose its Canvas course in course settings → **LMS**. Before you do, you can set that course's own
materials settings below it.

To stop syncing a course, turn off **Sync this course**. It then no longer updates and its Canvas
calendar events are hidden, but you can still read it in the Canvas tab and ask Sem about it.

## Automatic sync

Linked courses sync in the background, without you opening anything. Change how often in global
settings → **LMS → Sync**, or choose **Sync now** at any time. All sync settings are in global
settings → **LMS**; a course can set its own course material settings in course settings → **LMS**.

- **Imported assignments follow Canvas**: when a name, due date, weight or grade changes on Canvas,
  Semestra updates it. Values you changed yourself aren't overwritten; if both sides changed, you
  choose which to keep. To review a kind of change first, turn it off in global settings → **LMS →
  Assignments**.
- **New assignments wait for you**: new assignments on Canvas aren't added on their own; import or
  ignore them.
- **Assignments deleted on Canvas** are kept until you decide whether to delete them.
- **Course materials come into Files**: files, pages and recording links on Canvas appear in the
  course's files and follow Canvas there; new ones are marked until you open them. See
  [Course materials](/docs/lms/imports#course-materials).
- **Tasks complete when you submit**: when you submit an assignment on Canvas, its task is
  completed.

Sync status is shown at the bottom of the sidebar, under your Canvas's name: when it last ran, and
how many items are waiting for you. After **Sync now**, a progress bar shows how many courses are
done. Open it to see what needs you and the recent updates, course materials included. Assignment
updates from the last 14 days can be undone.

Values you changed that no longer match Canvas are listed separately, and each can go back to the
Canvas value in one step.

## Assignment details

Wherever an assignment shows up in Semestra, you can open its details right there: a row in the
Canvas tab, the button in the gradebook's **Assignment** column, the **Assignment
details** button on a task, a calendar item, a link in an announcement, an update in the sync
status, or a notification.

The details show the instructions, when it's due and how to submit, where you stand (not
submitted, submitted, or your score), what you submitted, and your instructor's comments and
rubric scores. From there you can jump to its row in the gradebook or its task, or add it to
both if you haven't imported it yet. Submitting still happens on Canvas: **Submit in Canvas**
takes you there.

An assignment stands the same way everywhere it appears: in its details, on its task, in the
Canvas tab and in Sem's answers. A graded assignment counts as graded even if it was marked missing,
an excused one says so, and one handed in on paper or not at all is shown as "No online submission",
never as due. An assignment is missing only when Canvas marks it so; one past its due date that
Canvas hasn't marked is still to do.

For imported assignments, all of this is saved in Semestra, so you can still open them when
Canvas can't be reached; Semestra tells you when what you see was saved.

## What else you get

- **Canvas tab**: read a course's announcements, assignments and grades, modules, pages, files and
  syllabus without leaving Semestra. Content uses easy-to-read typography by default, or Canvas's own
  style if you prefer.
- **Course links**: the course info card on a course's overview lists the tools the course uses on
  other sites, such as Piazza or Gradescope, as they appear in the course's Canvas menu.
- **Assignments and feedback in the gradebook**: each assessment from Canvas has a button in
  the **Assignment** column that opens the assignment's details; where your instructor left
  comments or rubric scores, it's the **View feedback** button and opens them. In the Canvas tab,
  an assignment's name underlines when you point at its row, and clicking the row opens it. They're saved in Semestra, so they stay readable after the course closes on
  Canvas. New comments appear under **Recent updates** in the sync status, and you get a
  notification. After you unbind an assessment or unlink the course, its feedback stays as it
  was, but no longer updates.
- **Calendar**: Canvas calendar events appear in your term calendar.
- **Announcements**: every linked course gets an **Announcements** page, and each term one that
  gathers the announcements of all its courses, with a filter by course. Unread announcements are
  marked, and the page's tab shows how many there are. Announcements are kept in Semestra, so you
  can read them even when Canvas can't be reached. Links to course files, assignments and pages
  open them in Semestra. Attachments that are in the course's files open in the file preview;
  others open in a new tab without going to Canvas. Choose how announcements look in global
  settings → **LMS → Content display**. With **Allow changes on the
  platform** on, what you read here is also marked read on Canvas, and the other way around.
  You can also search them in the command palette (⌘K, or Ctrl + K on Windows).
- **Announcements card**: keep the latest announcements on a course or term overview, with unread
  ones marked; click one to read it on the Announcements page.
- **Ask Sem**: Sem can search and read everything in a course on Canvas, including assignment
  instructions, rubrics and your instructor's comments, and tell you about new announcements, new
  grades and what's due. Files and pages already in the course's files are read from there, so Sem's
  answers cite them like any course file. Announcements and feedback saved in Semestra stay
  readable to Sem when Canvas can't be reached. Anything Sem brings in goes through an import you
  review first.

## Not supported yet

New Quizzes and discussion checkpoints can't be read yet.
