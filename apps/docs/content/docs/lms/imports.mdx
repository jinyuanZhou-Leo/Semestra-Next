---
title: Import from Canvas
description: Courses, assignments and syllabi go through a review before anything is written.
---

Every import from Canvas opens a review in Semestra's import center. Nothing is written until you
commit it, and you can edit or drop any item first.

## Courses

From the new-term wizard, or the command palette on a term page (**Import courses from LMS**).
Pick Canvas courses (the current term by default, or all), choose the target term, review, and
commit. Each course takes its title from the Canvas course name, its code from the Canvas course
code (also kept as an alias) and its website from the Canvas link, and is linked to its Canvas
course in the same step.

## Assignments

On a course's **Grades** page, open **Add** and choose the Canvas assignments under **From
existing material**. Pick
assignments, then decide per item whether it becomes a **gradebook assessment** or a **task**.

- As assessments, each Canvas assignment group becomes a grading category (with the group's
  weight when the course weights its groups), and the assignment's Canvas link is kept in its
  note.
- Weights come from Canvas assignment groups: an assessment's weight is its group's weight times
  its share of the group's points. When the course does not weight groups, the weight is left
  empty for you to fill in.
- Each assignment can be imported once. Imported assignments disappear from the list.

<Callout title="Grades are not imported">
  Assignment imports bring in names, due dates and weights, not points or your scores. Your Canvas
  grades are visible in the Canvas tab and to Sem, but the gradebook keeps only the scores you
  enter.
</Callout>

## Changes after an import

Imports are one-time copies, not a mirror. Semestra compares what you imported with Canvas, and
when a name or due date changes, or the weight of a weighted assessment changes (including
through its points), the Grades page and the Canvas tab show a "changed assignments" notice. Reviewing it opens another import review; your data changes only
when you commit.

## Syllabus

In the Canvas tab's syllabus view, send the Canvas syllabus to AI extraction. Course details,
grading scheme and deadlines are pulled out into a review, like an uploaded syllabus file. This
needs an [AI provider](/docs/ai).

## Files

Canvas files can be saved to the course's own files, where they are indexed for search and can
be used for extraction.
