---
title: Import from Canvas
description: Bring Canvas courses, assignments and syllabi into Semestra.
---

Course and syllabus imports open a review first and write nothing until you confirm. Assignment
imports are saved right away when there is nothing for you to decide; an assessment of the same
name already in the gradebook, or an assessment without a weight, opens the review. An assignment
named like an assessment you added yourself is bound to it there (**Bound to existing**): the
assessment is kept as it is and follows the assignment from then on. Choose **Add as a new
assessment** in that cell to import it separately instead. If you close the review and import the course's assignments again, you return to that
unfinished import, with any newly picked assignments added.

## Courses

From the new-term wizard, or a term's **Add course** menu (also in the command palette on a term
page), under **Import courses from LMS**.
Pick Canvas courses (the current term by default, or all), review, and
commit. Each course takes its title from the Canvas course name, its code from the Canvas course
code (also kept as an alias) and its website from the Canvas link, and is linked to its Canvas
course in the same step.

## Assignments

Either from a course's **Grades** page (open **Add** and choose the Canvas assignments under
**From existing material**), or from **Assignments & grades** in the Canvas tab: tick
assignments and choose **Import**. Assignments worth points become gradebook assessments (with a
to-do when they have a due date); assignments worth no points become to-dos. The **Semestra**
column says where each assignment went (**In gradebook** or **In to-dos**, linking there) or
**Not added**.

In the import review, the **Import to** column of an assignment is a menu: choose **To-dos** to bring an assignment worth points in as a to-do instead, or **Gradebook** the other way round.

When you open a program whose current-term courses have new assignments on Canvas (not imported
yet and not past due), a notification says "N new assignments on Canvas": **Import** brings them
in at once, and **Ignore** stops reminding you of these until new ones appear.

An assignment that is only a to-do can be moved with **Add to gradebook** in its row menu: it
becomes an assessment and keeps its to-do.

- As assessments, each Canvas assignment group goes into one of the course's grading categories:
  the one its earlier assignments are in now (so moving them in the gradebook teaches it), one of
  the same name or named inside the group's name, or a built-in category the name means
  ("Term Tests" → Exam, "Problem Sets" → Assignment). When the name says nothing ("Coursework",
  Canvas's default "Assignments"), the assignments inside decide if most of them agree. Only when
  nothing matches is a new category made from the group. The review shows where each group goes,
  and you can change it. The assignment's Canvas link is kept in its note.
- Weights come from Canvas assignment groups: an assessment's weight is its group's weight times
  its share of the group's points. When the course does not weight groups, the weight is left
  empty for you to fill in during the review.
- A grade you already have on Canvas comes along as the assessment's score (points earned of
  points possible).
- Each assignment is one thing in Semestra: an assessment (with its to-do) or a to-do.
- Importing only some of a group's assignments leaves the category's weight and its assessments'
  total apart; the rest can be imported later.

### Binding an assessment you added yourself

When an assessment you added by hand has a different name on Canvas, open its row menu on the
Grades page and choose **Bind Canvas assignment…**, then pick one of the course's assignments
not imported yet. Binding changes nothing in the assessment by itself: the next
[sync](./index.mdx#background-sync) fills in a due date, weight or grade it doesn't have yet, and
asks where it has one that differs from Canvas. From then on it behaves like an imported assessment
(it follows Canvas, and its to-do is finished when you hand in on Canvas).
**Unbind from Canvas** in the same menu keeps the assessment as it is and frees the assignment to
import or bind again; it works for imported assessments too.

## Changes after an import

Imported assignments follow Canvas through the [background sync](./index.mdx#background-sync):
a new name, due date, weight (including one that changes through the points) or grade on Canvas
is applied here unless you changed that value yourself, and anything both sides changed is asked
about. Each update can be undone from the sidebar's status line. With
[notifications](/docs/notifications) on, new grades and updates are also sent as a notification
while the app isn't open.

## Syllabus

In the Canvas tab's syllabus view, send the Canvas syllabus to AI extraction. Course details,
grading scheme and deadlines are pulled out into a review, like an uploaded syllabus file. This
needs an [AI provider](/docs/ai).

## Files

Canvas files can be saved to the course's own files, where they are indexed for search and can
be used for extraction.
