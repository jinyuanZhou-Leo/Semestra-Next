Semestra can show system notifications on your computer or phone, even when no Semestra tab is
open. Turn them on in **Settings → Notifications → This device**, or during
onboarding. Each browser and device is turned on separately; what you are notified about is
shared by all of them.

## What you are notified about

**Reminders** arrive at the time you pick, whether or not Semestra is open:

- **Classes**: when a class starts, or 5 to 30 minutes before.
- **Events**: one-off events, from when they start to a day before.
- **To-dos with a due time**: from the due time to a day before. To-dos due on a day without a
  time are counted in the daily summary instead.
- **Daily summary**: how many classes and to-dos today has, at an hour between 6 and 10 AM.
- **Habits**: turn on **Daily reminder** in a habit card's settings and pick a time; you are
  reminded then if today is not checked in yet (for a weekly goal: if the week's target is not
  reached yet).

**Activity** is only shown when no Semestra tab is open in front of you, since the app already
shows it otherwise:

- **Imports and background tasks**: an import is ready to review, or an export, restore or model
  download finishes or fails.
- **Canvas**: the background sync gave an imported assignment in a linked course a new grade, or
  updated its name, due date or weight; new announcements can be included too. Each update is told
  once. Grades and updates are sent only while the app isn't open, since it shows them itself;
  announcements are shown either way.
- **Sem approvals**: Sem is waiting for you to approve a change.
- **New sign-ins**: someone signs in to your account on another device. This one is shown even
  while the app is open.

Clicking a notification opens the page it is about. Times follow the time zone of your current
program. Changing a term's dates or breaks, or the program's time zone, moves the reminders
already planned at once.

## Quiet hours

With **Quiet hours → On a schedule** on, nothing is shown between the two times you set (for example 23:00 to
07:00). Reminders that fall inside that window are dropped, not held until morning.

## Turning notifications off

- To stop one kind, set it to **Off** under **Reminders** or turn it off under **Activity**
  (for Canvas, choose **Off**). A habit's reminder is turned off in that card's settings.
- To stop everything on one device, turn off its switch under **This device**.
- Signing out stops notifications on that device. Revoking a session under **Settings → Account**
  does the same for the device it belongs to.

If the browser blocked notifications, Semestra cannot ask again: allow them for this site in the
browser's site settings.

## iPhone and iPad

Safari only shows web notifications from an app added to the Home Screen. Add Semestra from the
**Share** menu (see [Using Semestra on a phone](/docs/mobile)), open it from the Home Screen,
sign in, and turn notifications on there.
