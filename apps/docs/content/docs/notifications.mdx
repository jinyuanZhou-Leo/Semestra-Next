Semestra can show system notifications on your computer or phone, even when Semestra isn't open.
Turn them on for a device in Settings → **Notifications**. Each browser and device is turned on
separately; what you're notified about is shared by all of them.

## Reminders

Reminders arrive at the time you choose, whether or not Semestra is open:

- **Classes**: when a class starts, or a few minutes before.
- **Events**: when an event starts, or some time before.
- **Tasks with a due time**: at the due time, or some time before. Tasks due on a day without a
  time are counted in the daily summary instead.
- **Daily summary**: each morning, how many classes and tasks you have today.
- **Habits**: turn on a daily reminder in a habit card's settings; you're reminded only if you
  haven't checked in yet.

## Activity

These are only sent when Semestra isn't open, since the app shows them otherwise:

- **Imports and background jobs**: an import is ready to review, or an export or restore finishes
  or fails.
- **LMS**: an imported assignment got a new grade, or its name or due date changed. You can also be
  told about new announcements.
- **Sem approvals**: Sem is waiting for you to approve a change.
- **New sign-ins**: someone signed in to your account on another device. This one is sent even
  while the app is open.

Select a notification to open the page it's about.

## Quiet hours

With **Quiet hours** on, nothing is shown during the times you set (for example 23:00 to 07:00).
Reminders that fall in that window are skipped; LMS activity is held until quiet hours end.

## Turn notifications off

- To stop one kind, turn it off in settings. A habit's reminder is turned off in that card's
  settings.
- To stop everything on one device, turn off that device's switch.
- Signing out stops notifications on that device.

If your browser blocked notifications, allow them for Semestra in the browser's site settings.

## iPhone and iPad

On iPhone and iPad, only Semestra added to the Home Screen can receive notifications. First
[add it to your Home Screen](/docs/mobile#add-to-home-screen), open it from there and sign in, then
turn notifications on.
