A term's **Calendar** page shows three kinds of things: classes, events (one-off events you add
and your LMS's calendar events) and to-dos with a due date. An assessment with a due date always
has a to-do, so its deadline appears once, as that to-do.

- Tick a to-do's checkbox to finish it. It stays on its day, faded, and is finished on the
  **To-dos** page too.
- To-dos are deadlines, so the week view lists them in the all-day row, a timed one with its
  time in front.
- Drag a to-do to another day; it keeps its time. In the month view you can also drop it on
  another time. The to-do of an assessment takes its date from the gradebook, so change it there.
- **New**, or selecting a time slot, creates an **Event** or a **To-do** due then.
- Open an assessment's to-do and choose **Open** to go to its row in the course's grades.

## Export

The **Export** menu in the toolbar has three choices.

### Image or PDF

Saves the week view as a PNG or an A4 PDF, for printing or sending to someone. Pick one week, a
range of weeks, or a weekly timetable without dates; choose what to include, how cancelled
classes look, and a light or dark theme.

### Calendar file (.ics)

Downloads the term (or the weeks you pick) as a `.ics` file to import once into Apple Calendar,
Google Calendar or Outlook. Courses hidden in the calendar's filters are left out. Later changes
in Semestra do not reach the imported copy; use a subscription link for that.

### Subscription link

Each program has one read-only subscription link. Calendar apps check it on their own schedule
(usually every few hours), so changes in Semestra show up without doing anything.

- **Turn on** the link the first time you open it. After that it is always shown here: copy it,
  open it in your calendar app, or add it to Google Calendar.
- **Include** classes and events, to-dos (assessment due dates among them) and LMS calendar
  items. Finished to-dos are left out. Changes apply to the same link.
- **Reset link** gives it a new address if it was shared by mistake; calendars subscribed to the
  old one stop updating and need the new link.
- **Turn off link** stops it for every calendar that uses it.

Anyone with the link can see what it includes, so share it only with calendars you own.
