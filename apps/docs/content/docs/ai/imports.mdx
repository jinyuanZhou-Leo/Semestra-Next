---
title: Import from files
description: Read courses, assessments and meetings from a syllabus, a timetable screenshot or a calendar file, and review them before anything is saved.
---

Instead of entering everything by hand, give Semestra a syllabus, a timetable screenshot or a
calendar file. It lists what it found, you review it, and only what you keep is imported. Reading a
syllabus or a screenshot needs a [provider](./index.mdx); calendar files (`.ics`) don't need AI.

## What you can import

| Source                              | What it brings in                                  | Where to start                         |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| A syllabus                          | The grade breakdown, assessments, weights and due dates | A course's gradebook                   |
| A calendar file or timetable screenshot | Weekly meetings                                | A course's schedule                    |
| Syllabi, a screenshot, a calendar file or pasted text | All of a term's courses and what belongs to them | Creating a term, or adding courses to one |
| A syllabus                          | Course code, title, instructors, credits and website | Course settings → **General → Read from a file** |

To import courses and assignments from your LMS, see [Import from your LMS](/docs/lms/imports).

Upload a new file, or pick a syllabus already in the course's files. An uploaded syllabus is also
saved to the course's files unless you choose otherwise.

## Review before importing

When reading finishes, the review opens. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

- Each item shows where it will go: the gradebook, tasks, the schedule or the calendar.
- Anything worth checking is marked, such as a date that couldn't be read or an assessment the
  gradebook already has.
- Edit any item, and uncheck what you don't want.
- Each course shows its total weight after the import, so you can check it adds up to 100%.

Dates written relative to the term, such as “Friday of week 5”, are worked out from the course's
term. Importing the same syllabus again doesn't add its tasks and events twice.

Closing the review doesn't discard the import: reading carries on in the background, and opening
the import from the same place brings you back to it.

## Calendar files

Only dates inside the target term are read, so a full export from your calendar app works. Classes
that meet every other week are recognized even with a reading week in between, and weeks missing
from the file become cancelled classes.
