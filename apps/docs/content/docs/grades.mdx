## A course's grades

The **Grades** tab of a course lists its assessments. Type a score straight into the **Score**
column: `82` or `82%` is a percentage, `18/20` is points. Weights are typed the same way.

Every assessment with a due date has a to-do that follows it: its title, due date and course come
from the assessment, and it shows in the calendar and on the **To-dos** page. Entering a score,
or dropping the assessment, finishes the to-do; deleting the assessment deletes it. The **To-do**
column checks it off right from the table, and the to-do's details show the assessment's weight
and score with a link back to its row.

Above the table, show **All**, **Graded** or **Not graded** assessments, or search
them by name. **Add** creates an assessment, or imports them from a syllabus or your LMS. The
**⋯** menu groups the table by category and holds the official grade.

On the right:

- **Grade breakdown**: the current grade and GPA, and each category's weight and average.
- **Forecast**: the predicted final grade, with a bar for each assessment still to come. Each one
  is predicted from its category's average so far; a category with no scores yet uses the
  course's average. The dashed line is the average the target needs.
- **Target GPA**: pick the grade you are aiming for from your program's scale. Below it is the
  average the remaining assessments need to reach it.

## Plan mode

**Plan** lets you try out scores without saving them. Empty assessments show their forecast in
grey; type a score to replace it. The forecast card turns into the **Planned grade**.
**Fill in to reach target** fills in scores that just reach your target. **Clear assumptions** removes
what you typed, and **Exit plan mode** goes back to your real grades. Assumptions are kept for next
time.

## A term's grades

The term's **Grades** page lists its courses with their percentage and GPA. Filter **In
progress** or **Finished** courses, or search them. The **Unfinished courses** card shows each
unfinished course's average so far. Enter a **Target term GPA** to see the average the unfinished
courses need. **Plan** works the same way as for a course: type the final percentage you expect
in each unfinished course.

Whether unfinished courses count in the term GPA at their current average is a program setting:
program settings → **Grades → Term grades → Count unfinished courses**.

A course that runs over two or more terms in a row (a full-year course, say) has one final grade.
Turn on **Year-long course** in course settings → **General**, then pick the terms it starts and ends
in; if the last term is not added yet, pick **Next term (not added yet)**.
