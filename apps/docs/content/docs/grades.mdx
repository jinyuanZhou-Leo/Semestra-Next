Each course's gradebook lists all its assessments. As you enter scores, Semestra works out your
current grade and GPA, forecasts your final grade, and tells you what you still need to reach
your target.

## Enter scores

Type scores straight into the gradebook: `82` or `82%` is a percentage, `18/20` is points. Weights
work the same way.

Add assessments yourself, or import them from a [syllabus](/docs/ai/imports) or your
[LMS](/docs/lms/imports). An assessment with a due date gets a matching task that shows in your
calendar and tasks, and the task is completed when you enter a score.

## Change several assessments at once

Select several assessments to change their category, weight or whether they count, or to delete
them together. You can give each the same weight, or split one total weight evenly among them.

## See where a course is heading

- **Grade breakdown**: your current grade and GPA, and each category's weight and average.
- **Forecast**: your likely final grade based on how you're doing, with an estimate for each
  assessment still to come. Categories with few scores lean on the course average, so one lucky or
  unlucky score doesn't decide a whole category.
- **Target GPA**: pick a target to see the average the remaining assessments need.

## Plan the rest of your grades

Turn on **Plan** to try out scores for assessments that aren't graded yet and see how your final
grade changes, without touching your real grades. **Fill in to reach target** fills in scores that
just reach your target. Your planned scores are kept for next time.

## Term grades

A term's grades page lists each course's grade and GPA. Enter a **Target term GPA** to see the
average your unfinished courses need, or use **Plan** to enter the final grade you expect in each.

Whether unfinished courses count toward the term GPA at their current grade is set in program
settings → **Grades** (**Count unfinished courses**).

## Courses that span terms

A course that runs over two or more terms in a row, such as a full-year course, has one final
grade. Turn on **Year-long course** in course settings → **General**, then choose the terms it starts
and ends in.

## Hide grades

If you'd rather not see your grades every time you open the app, turn on **Hide grades** in program
settings → **Grades**. GPA and percentages show as “•••”; point to one to see it. Scores on
individual assessments stay visible.
