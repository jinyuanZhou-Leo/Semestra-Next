Plugins add extra features to Semestra, mostly cards you can place on a page. Every plugin comes
from Semestra. You install them for your account, and they're available in all your programs.

## Installing and uninstalling

In **Settings → Plugins**, plugins are grouped into **Installed** and **Not installed**, and you can
search them by name or description. Click **Install** to add one; click a plugin to see the pages
and cards it includes, along with its own settings.

After installing a card plugin, add it to a board from the page's card editing.

To uninstall, click **⋯** at the top right of the plugin and choose **Uninstall**. Its pages and
cards disappear and come back if you reinstall it. If you check **Also delete the plugin's data**, its
cards, settings and records are deleted for good.
