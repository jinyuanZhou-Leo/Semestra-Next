Enter your email on the sign-in page and Semestra sends you a code. In settings → **Account** you
can also set a password, link a Google account or add passkeys to sign in with next time.

## Passkeys

A passkey signs you in with your fingerprint, face or device passcode. No password to type, and
look-alike sites can't use it.

1. Open settings → **Account** → **Passkeys** and click **Add passkey**.
2. Click **Continue** and follow your browser to save it, for example to iCloud Keychain or Google
   Password Manager.

Next time, click **Sign in with a passkey** on the sign-in page, or click the email field and pick
it from your browser's suggestions. Passkeys saved in a password manager sync to your other devices.

You can rename or delete passkeys in the list. A deleted passkey can no longer sign in; remove it
from your password manager too.

## Confirming it's you

Before changing your email or password, adding a passkey or deleting your account, Semestra asks
you to confirm it's you if your last sign-in or check was over 10 minutes ago. It starts with the
easiest way, such as a passkey; **Use another way** switches to your password, Google or an email
code. After that it won't ask again for 10 minutes.
