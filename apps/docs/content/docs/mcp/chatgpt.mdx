ChatGPT connects to Semestra by signing in, with no API token.

## Connect

1. In Semestra, open **AI** in settings, go to **API & MCP**, click **New connection**, choose **ChatGPT** and copy the server address.
2. In ChatGPT, open **Plugins**, click **+** and choose **Add custom MCP server**.
3. Name it Semestra and paste the address. If asked for an authentication method, choose OAuth. Confirm the notice, then create it.
4. Your browser opens Semestra's consent page. Sign in if you need to, choose **Read only** or **Read and write**, then click **Allow**.
5. You're taken back to ChatGPT, connected. Semestra's window shows “Connected” too.

## Access

- **Read only**: ChatGPT can view your courses, schedule, grades, tasks and files.
- **Read and write**: it can also add and change things. Deleting data needs its own switch under **Client permissions**.

ChatGPT asks you to confirm changes in the chat before making them. To change the access, disconnect and connect again.

## Disconnect

In Semestra, find ChatGPT under **Clients and API tokens**, click **⋯** and choose **Disconnect**. ChatGPT loses access right away. You can also delete it from ChatGPT's plugins. A connection unused for 90 days disconnects on its own.

## Notes

- Whether you can add a custom MCP server depends on your ChatGPT plan and workspace settings.
- ChatGPT connects to Semestra from its own servers, so a self-hosted Semestra must be reachable from the internet over HTTPS. Until it is, **ChatGPT** doesn't appear under **New connection**. If you only use it on a local network, use a client on your computer such as [Codex](./codex.mdx).
