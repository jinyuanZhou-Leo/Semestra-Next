Claude connects to Semestra by signing in, with no API token. Add it once and it works in Claude on the web, desktop and phone.

## Connect

1. In Semestra, open **AI** in settings, go to **API & MCP**, click **New connection**, choose **Claude** and copy the server address.
2. In Claude, open **Customize**, go to **Connectors** and click **Add custom connector**.
3. Paste the address, click **Add**, then **Connect**.
4. Your browser opens Semestra's consent page. Sign in if you need to, choose **Read only** or **Read and write**, then click **Allow**.
5. You're taken back to Claude, connected. Semestra's window shows “Connected” too.

## Access

- **Read only**: Claude can view your courses, schedule, grades, tasks and files.
- **Read and write**: it can also add and change things. Deleting data needs its own switch under **Client permissions**.

Claude asks you to approve tool calls according to its own settings. To change the access, disconnect and connect again.

## Disconnect

In Semestra, find Claude under **Clients and API tokens**, click **⋯** and choose **Disconnect**. Claude loses access right away. You can also remove it from Claude's connectors. A connection unused for 90 days disconnects on its own.

## Notes

- The Free plan can add one custom connector. On Team and Enterprise, an organization owner adds it under **Connectors** in organization settings, then each member connects.
- Claude connects to Semestra from its own servers, so a self-hosted Semestra must be reachable from the internet over HTTPS. Until it is, **Claude** doesn't appear under **New connection**. If you only use it on a local network, use a client on your computer such as [Claude Code](./claude-code.mdx).
