When a teacher turns off an area on Canvas, its materials are no longer treated as deleted. They are listed under “Not syncing for now” in the sync status, where you can keep saved copies as your own files or remove the rest, one by one or all at once.
<!-- zh -->
老师在 Canvas 上关闭某个栏目后，其中的资料不再被当作已删除，而是列在同步状态的“暂时无法同步”里，可以逐项或一次性把已保存的资料转为你自己的文件，或移除其余资料。
